Privacy Policy — Smart Waste

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Smart Waste is a mobile application designed to help users identify waste materials and learn how to dispose of them correctly.

For privacy-related questions, contact:

janetadedosu818@gmail.com

2. Camera Access

Smart Waste requests access to your device's camera to capture images of waste materials for classification.

Images are analyzed locally on your device using a TensorFlow Lite machine-learning model.

Smart Waste does not intentionally save captured images to your photo gallery or upload them to an external classification server.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

Smart Waste requests microphone access to support voice commands and speech recognition.

Speech recognition uses services available on your device. Depending on your device and its speech recognition provider, audio or speech data may be processed by that provider.

The provider's privacy practices may apply to this processing.

4. Machine-Learning Processing

Smart Waste uses an on-device machine-learning model to classify waste materials and provide disposal guidance.

Image classification does not require uploading captured images to an external server.

5. User Accounts

Smart Waste does not require users to create an account or provide personal registration information.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Smart Waste does not intentionally maintain a database of captured waste images or user accounts.

Images may be held temporarily during capture and processing. Any temporary files created by the application or its underlying frameworks are subject to device storage and cleanup behavior.

7. Third-Party Services

Smart Waste uses software libraries and device services to provide camera access, speech recognition, and machine-learning functionality.

Some device services, particularly speech recognition, may operate under the privacy practices of their respective providers.

8. Children's Privacy

Smart Waste is intended as an educational waste-sorting application. It does not require children to submit personal information to create an account.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if Smart Waste's functionality or data-handling practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
