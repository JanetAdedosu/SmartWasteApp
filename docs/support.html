Support & Help Center

Welcome

Smart Waste App helps you identify common household waste categories using image recognition. This page provides help with using the app and resolving common issues.

How to Use Smart Waste

  1. Open Smart Waste App.
  2. Allow camera access when requested.
  3. Point the camera at a waste item.
  4. Tap Scan.
  5. Review the suggested waste category.

The app supports Biotonne, Gelbe Tonne, Glas, Papier, and Restmüll.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the camera not working?

Check your device settings and ensure camera permission is enabled for Smart Waste App. Restart the app if necessary.

Why is the prediction incorrect?

AI predictions are not always accurate. Try scanning the item again with better lighting and a clear background. Always follow local waste disposal guidelines.

Why is voice recognition not working?

Check microphone and speech recognition permissions in your device settings. Make sure your device supports speech recognition.

Does Smart Waste replace official recycling guidance?

No. Smart Waste is an educational assistance tool. Local recycling rules may vary by municipality.
